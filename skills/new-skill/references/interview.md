# Interview

At most 5 questions, one at a time, each with your recommended answer. Outcomes, not technology.

1. **The job.** "What do you keep asking your agent to do, or wish it did without being told?" Recommend the most repeated thing you saw in their chats, if you read them.
2. **Who uses it.** Just them, their team, or anyone who installs it? This decides how plain the wording must be and whether it gets published.
3. **The words.** "What would you type when you want this?" Collect 5 to 10 real phrases. They become the trigger sentence in the description ("Use for ..."). Add the obvious synonyms yourself.
4. **Done.** "How do you know it worked?" This becomes the "Done when" section and the tests.
5. **Never.** "What must it never do?" (spend money, push to main, delete, send messages, read keys). These become rules.

Skip a question when the answer is already clear from what they said or from their code. Write the spec yourself; do not make them write it.
